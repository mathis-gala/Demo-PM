## 23. Translation Stress Tests

- Do not translate brand names such as Bosch, Brembo, Michelin, Castrol, Thule, K&N, WeatherTech, and PowerStop.
- Preserve SKU codes exactly: BRK-4589-F, OIL-5W30-946ML, LED-H11-6500K.
- Preserve placeholders exactly: {{vehicle_make}}, {{order_number}}, {{discount_percent}}.
- Preserve HTML tags: <strong>Free shipping</strong> on eligible orders.
- Preserve markdown links: [View return policy](/returns).
- Preserve legal wording carefully without adding promises.
- Preserve capitalization for technical acronyms: OEM, VIN, SKU, OBD-II, EV, AGM, LED, ABS, EGR, DPF, TPMS.
- Translate customer-facing copy naturally, not word-for-word.
- Keep CTA text short and action-oriented.
- Avoid overly casual slang in transactional or legal content.
- Keep measurement units unchanged unless explicitly requested.
- For French, use a professional commercial tone and avoid literal translations such as “garage” when it means saved vehicles, unless the site intentionally uses that metaphor.
